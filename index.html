Debut title · Rhythm Swimmin’

Eight lanes. One clock.
No luck in it.

You race the field and you race the tenths. The pool does not care how hard you try — only whether you arrived on the beat.

The twist

Tapping faster
makes you slower.

Mash the button and the stroke breaks. Rush the breath and the line collapses. Speed is not input rate — it is the interval you keep between inputs.

The strokes

Three strokes.
Three different clocks.

Each one is a separate rhythm with its own way of going wrong. Freestyle is the quickest of the three, breaststroke the slowest, butterfly between them — the same order they sit in in the water.

  1. 01

    Freestyle

    Left, then right, evenly spaced. The tempo readout is the judge — not your thumbs.

    Fails ona beat you can’t hold for the length

  2. 02

    Butterfly

    Both arms together, two kicks to a cycle: one with the pull, one between pulls.

    Fails ona single, un-synced arm — an illegal stroke

  3. 03

    Breaststroke

    Pull, kick, glide — strictly in that order. The glide is where the free speed is.

    Fails onpulling again before the glide is spent
