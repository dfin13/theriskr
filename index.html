01 — The studio

THERISKR makes games
about timing.

An independent studio building games where the interesting decision is when, not how fast. Small, deliberate, finished — no systems for the sake of systems.

Everything ships playable first and gets sharpened in the open, with the people playing it.

02 — Debut title · Rhythm Swimmin’

Eight lanes. One clock.
No luck in it.

You race the field and you race the tenths. The pool does not care how hard you try — only whether you arrived on the beat.

The twist

Tapping faster
makes you slower.

Mash the button and the stroke breaks. Rush the breath and the line collapses. Speed is not input rate — it is the interval you keep between inputs.

The four strokes

Four rhythms.
Four different races.

  1. I

    Freestyle

    Relentless. The tempo you can actually hold for the length.

  2. II

    Backstroke

    Blind to the wall. Count, trust the flags, commit to the turn.

  3. III

    Breaststroke

    Glide is free speed. Every extra pull costs you distance.

  4. IV

    Butterfly

    Two kicks, one breath, no mercy. The rhythm that punishes greed.

The career

Swim a season.
Hang the times.

Meets, heats, and personal bests that follow you. Every race is written to the board in hundredths — yours, and everyone else’s.
